Student Housing in Sault Ste. Marie

Comfortable, fully-furnished rooms designed for students attending Sault College and Algoma University.

Why Choose Soo Suites?

Finding the right place to live is one of the most important decisions you'll make as a student. At Soo Suites, we've designed our accommodations specifically with students in mind — whether you're coming from across Ontario or across the world.

Just a 2-minute walk to Sault College. Our location means you can sleep in a little longer, save on transportation, and still make it to class on time. Algoma University is also easily accessible by bus or a short drive.

Room Options

We offer two room types to suit different budgets and preferences. Our Master Bedroom is a spacious private room ideal for students who want extra space and privacy. Our North/South Rooms are cozy, well-lit rooms perfect for students who prefer a more compact and affordable option. Both come fully furnished with a bed, desk, and storage.

The Neighbourhood

Sault Ste. Marie is a welcoming, safe community in Northern Ontario situated on the St. Marys River. The city offers affordable living compared to larger Canadian cities, with easy access to grocery stores, restaurants, parks, and transit. Students enjoy outdoor activities year-round, from hiking and kayaking in summer to skiing and snowshoeing in winter.

For International Students

Moving to a new country is a big step, and we're here to make the transition easier. Many of our tenants are international students, and we understand the unique challenges you face. Our accommodations provide a stable, comfortable home base so you can focus on your studies and settling into life in Canada.
